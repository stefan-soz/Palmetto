RV Issues Keeping You Stuck?

Troubleshoot on-site without the hassle of towing.

Air conditioning cutting out during a hot South Carolina afternoon, water leaks under the sink, intermittent power drops, or a slide-out that won't pull back in before checkout—dealing with RV component failures is frustrating. Hooking up and towing a rig to a service facility just to figure out what is wrong takes time and extra expense.

Palmetto RV and Camper Mobile Service comes directly to where your RV is parked in West Columbia and surrounding areas. We inspect internal systems, identify the fault, and explain your options on-site so you can make informed decisions without hauling your unit to a dealership.
